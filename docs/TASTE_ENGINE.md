## 🧠 TASTE RULES (HARD ENFORCED)

Before outputting ANY UI, you must score it internally:

**A. Hierarchy**
- Is there a clear visual priority?
- Can user understand what matters in 3 seconds?

**B. Density**
- Is the screen overcrowded?
- Are there unnecessary elements?

**C. Consistency**
- Are spacing, radius, and typography consistent?

**D. Component discipline**
- Are you reusing known components instead of inventing new ones?

**E. SaaS realism**
- Would this feel at home in Vercel, Linear, Notion, Stripe-level UI?
